Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a single finished application icon for Codex Relay, a developer API relay and bridge.
Primary request: Create a distinctive, carefully art-directed icon with an original recognizable silhouette. The user rejected an ordinary white R on a blue square as too generic.
Concept: THE FOLDED RELAY GATE. Two very broad opposing folded ribbons, one porcelain white and one vivid ice cyan, interleave into one compact angular gateway. An unmistakable wide diagonal passage of negative space cuts through the middle. The symbol communicates bridging and transferring a request through a portal. Make it a proprietary abstract emblem, not a letter. Use exactly two main ribbon forms, beautifully balanced asymmetric geometry, chamfered turns and broad cut ends. Wide openings and bold masses must remain readable at 24px and 34px.
Style: premium industrial graphic design with restrained sculptural planar depth; clean broad faces and only one or two clearly defined folded side faces. Crisp, precise, solid edges; simplicity and visual force, not ornamental detail or photorealistic texture.
Tile: rounded squircle in saturated petrol teal-blue, bright enough to stand out on a dark sidebar, with a very subtle tonal shift. White and vivid ice cyan ribbons have strong contrast against the tile. No black muddy background. The symbol occupies about 76% of the tile. Tile fills almost the whole image with about 3% transparent breathing room. Front-on view, no tilted perspective.
Composition: one centered square icon only, fully finished and ready to use. Transparent outside the tile; preserve actual alpha.
Avoid: typography, letters R or C, any text or watermark, ordinary letter monograms, generic arrows, sync arrows, chain links, rings, network nodes, circuits, sparkles, OpenAI knot, borders with tiny details, glow, blurry shadows, delicate lines, busy extrusions, glossy plastic, mockups, contact sheets, variants.
